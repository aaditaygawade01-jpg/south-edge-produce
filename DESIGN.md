# Design System: South Edge Produce

## Style Reference
Premium olive oil brand meets Tasmanian wilderness editorial photography.
Think: Cobram Estate, Cape Grim Beef, premium wine label websites.
Clean, confident, sophisticated. NOT rustic. NOT farmy. NOT corporate.

## Colors
- Primary Olive: #3D4A2E, #5C6B3E, #8A9B6E, #B8C9A0
- Backgrounds: #FAFAF7 (Warm White), #F2F0EB (Cream), #E8E4DC, #D9D3C7
- Dark Accents: #1C2018 (Charcoal Deep), #2E3528, #4A5240
- Gradients: 
  - Hero: linear-gradient(to bottom, rgba(28,32,24,0.40) 0%, rgba(28,32,24,0.65) 55%, rgba(28,32,24,0.82) 100%)
  - Olive: linear-gradient(135deg, #3D4A2E, #5C6B3E, #8A9B6E)

## Typography
- Headings: "Cormorant Garamond" (Elegant Serif)
- Body: "Inter" (Clean Sans-Serif)

## Components
- Buttons: Corner radius 4px, background #3D4A2E (Olive Deep)
- Navbar: Sticky, background rgba(250,250,247,0.96) with backdrop-filter blur 16px.
- Hero: Dark overlay MANDATORY (rgba(28,32,24,0.60) minimum).

## Images
- Dramatic Tasmanian landscapes (misty hills, green fields).
- Actual shallots (golden and red).
- Black garlic (dark, moody).
- Editorial and premium feel.
